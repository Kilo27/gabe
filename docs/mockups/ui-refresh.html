Recommendation

A1 · Colour-coded. It keeps Gabe's colour map, so every area is still recognisable at a glance, but the colour sits on small marks against a white, ruled page. That reads as more professional than today, and the rows that need a person still stand out.

A2 is the safer step if people are attached to the current tiles. It's louder, and with eight hues filled at once in the nav, the pink “needs you” rows have more to compete with.
