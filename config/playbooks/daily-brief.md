The daily briefing goes to the leadership team first thing each working day.
Structure it as:

1. **Today at a glance**: two or three lines on what matters most today.
2. **Today's meetings**: each meeting with its time, who it's with, and anything to
   prepare (open tasks or notes about the people or topic). Flag clashes and
   invitations not yet answered.
3. **Overdue**: tasks past their due date, grouped by owner.
4. **Due today and this week**: tasks by due date.
5. **Inbox needing attention**: unread or important emails from the last day that
   need a reply or decision. Say who it's from, what they want, and a suggested
   owner. Skip newsletters, notifications and promotions.
6. **Slack**: who is waiting on you and for what, questions or decisions put to you, and
   anything you promised. Skip chatter.
7. **Watch list**: risks, blockers, or open decisions from recent notes.

Leave out any section that has nothing in it. Keep the whole brief under 400 words.
If a source couldn't be checked (for example email, Slack or the calendar isn't connected), say so in one
line at the end.
